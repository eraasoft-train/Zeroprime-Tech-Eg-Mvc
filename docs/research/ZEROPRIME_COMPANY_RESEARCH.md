**Product Description:**

CanBank is a reverse vending machine designed to encourage recycling by providing users with immediate rewards. The system accepts used beverage containers including aluminum cans and plastic bottles. Users receive instant benefits such as mobile phone credit or promotional codes. Alternatively, users can opt to donate their rewards to charitable organizations.
